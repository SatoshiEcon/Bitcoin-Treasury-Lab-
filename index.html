Step 2: Get the prices, and their dates

What's happening: the market value of the treasury moves every second with the bitcoin price, even though nothing in the company changed. That is the core risk the finance team has to manage and report.

Match your dates. Your holdings are from the filing date. If you use today's prices, mNAV compares today's market against old holdings. The cleanest approach is to use the share price and bitcoin price on the filing "as of" date (any historical price chart works). If you use today's prices, say so in your answers and explain how the gap could affect your numbers.

The Treasury yield measures what the company gave up: the interest it could have earned holding cash in Treasury bills instead of bitcoin.

Step 3: The metrics

What's happening: these are the metrics bitcoin treasury companies report, plus a few they don't. mNAV is market cap divided by the market value of the bitcoin. Above 1, investors pay a premium for each dollar of bitcoin the company holds, which lets it issue stock to buy more. Bitcoin yield is the percent change in bitcoin per diluted share. It is a company KPI, not a yield in the bond sense, and it ignores debt and preferred claims. That's why the table also shows bitcoin per share net of debt and preferred: what's left for shareholders after the creditors and preferred holders are paid.

Step 4: Scenario test (down and up)

What's happening: bitcoin has fallen more than 50% several times, and it has also more than doubled in a single year. A CFO shows the board both. On the downside, the question is whether the company can still cover its debt, interest and preferred dividends, and whether near-term maturities or collateral calls could force it to sell. On the upside, fair-value accounting puts the gain straight into reported earnings. That swings net income, can create tax and covenant questions, and tends to push the mNAV premium up, which tempts management to issue more stock and buy more bitcoin.

The share price won't sit still. Treasury company stocks usually move more than bitcoin, and the mNAV premium can shrink or vanish in a sell-off. Use the beta below to test that: at 1.5, a 50% bitcoin drop takes the share price down 75%.
